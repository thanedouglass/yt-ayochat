where this belongs

Native infrastructure, not an extension.

A creator who answers comments to build a community is doing the platform's own job. That work should not require a third-party add-on, an API key of their own, and a browser tab nobody asked them to keep open.

Every comment-to-DM tool on the market today is scaffolding bolted to the outside of YouTube Studio. Creators paste tokens between dashboards, grant scopes they cannot audit, and hand the relationship they are trying to own to whichever vendor sits in the middle.

The retrieval, the governance and the reply already run on Alphabet infrastructure. Vertex AI serves the model. Model Armor screens the prompt. Sensitive Data Protection redacts before generation. The pipeline never leaves the ecosystem, so the control surface should not either.

Build on top of this.

The governance layer is the interesting part, and it is the part most likely to be wrong for a language or a community it was never tested against. Fork it, measure it, and push the policy further than this repo took it.

  1. 01

    Clone the pipeline

    The listener, gateway, retrieval and guardrail stages are separate modules. Read the one you want to change before you change it.

  2. 02

    Run the evaluation gate

    Nine benchmark cases run against the golden dataset with custom metrics. Get a passing baseline on your machine before you touch the policy, so you can tell your regressions from ours.

  3. 03

    Extend the Semantic Governance Policy

    The SGP intercepts prompt injections and redacts personal data before generation. Its screening was written and benchmarked in English. Low-resource languages need their own cases in the golden dataset and their own thresholds, and that contribution is worth more to this project than another feature.
